Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Step 1: Download the Tapo App

Download the free Tapo app (available for iOS and Android) and create or log into your TP-Link ID. The entire setup process is handled through the app — no computer or separate software needed.

Step 2: Charge the Battery Before Mounting

Before installing, charge the internal battery fully using the included USB-C cable. This ensures the camera is ready to operate immediately and gives you time to verify the camera works before committing to a mounting location.

Step 3: Choose the Right Mounting Location

Select a location that gets direct sunlight for several hours per day to maximize solar charging. Aim the camera at the area you want to monitor — entry points, driveways, and side gates are prime locations. Make sure it's within range of your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network.

Step 4: Mount the Camera and Solar Panel

Use the included hardware to mount the camera bracket to your wall, eave, or fence. Angle the solar panel toward the sun (south-facing in the northern hemisphere) for maximum power generation. The solar panel cable connects directly to the camera's charging port.

Step 5: Connect to Wi-Fi via the Tapo App

In the Tapo app, tap the "+" button to add a new device. Select your camera model, follow the on-screen pairing instructions, and connect it to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. The process typically takes less than 5 minutes.

Step 6: Configure Detection Zones and Alerts

Set up custom motion detection zones to focus on specific areas and reduce false alerts. Configure notification preferences — choose to be alerted for people, vehicles, pets, or all motion. Adjust sensitivity to suit your environment.

Step 7: Set Up Storage

Insert a microSD card (sold separately, up to 512GB) for local recording. Optionally activate Tapo Care cloud storage for remote access to recorded clips and extended history. The camera supports both simultaneously.
