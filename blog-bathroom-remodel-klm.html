Key Renovation Ideas Featured

Walk-In Shower Conversion

Converting a dated tub/shower combo into a walk-in shower opens up the bathroom space and creates a modern, accessible look. This is one of the most popular and impactful bathroom upgrades homeowners can make.

Custom Cabinetry

Replacing builder-grade vanities with custom cabinetry transforms the entire feel of a bathroom. Custom cabinets allow you to choose the exact size, finish, and storage configuration that fits your space and style.

Large-Format Floor Tiles

Installing large-format floor tiles creates a spacious, modern look with fewer grout lines. This design choice makes bathrooms feel bigger and is easier to maintain over time.

Built-In Shower Storage

Adding recessed niches and built-in storage to shower walls eliminates the need for hanging caddies and keeps the shower looking clean and organized.

Upgraded Lighting

Upgrading lighting from basic overhead fixtures to layered, spa-like lighting dramatically elevates the bathroom experience. Consider vanity sconces, recessed ceiling lights, and accent lighting for a complete transformation.

Planning Your Bathroom Remodel

Step-by-Step Planning Guide

  • 💰 Set a realistic budget — include a 10-15% contingency for surprises
  • 🔨 Decide what stays and what goes — full gut vs. surface refresh
  • 🎨 Choose a tile and fixture style that complements your home
  • 👷 Hire licensed contractors for plumbing, electrical, and tile work
  • 📅 Plan for the timeline — a full bathroom remodel typically takes 2–4 weeks
