How to Size Your Solar Power System

▶
Watch on YouTube

Sizing a solar power system correctly is the most important step before you spend a dollar on panels or batteries. Too small and you won't meet your energy needs. Too large and you overpay. This guide walks through the exact process for calculating the right system size for your home.

Step 1: Calculate Your Daily Energy Consumption

Review your electricity bills for the past 12 months. Find your average daily kWh usage by dividing your monthly kWh by 30. Account for seasonal variation — summer AC loads vs. winter heating will significantly affect your average.

Step 2: Determine Your Peak Sun Hours

Peak sun hours vary by location, typically 4–6 hours per day in the US. Use a solar irradiance map or a tool like PVWatts to find your area's average. This number directly affects how many panels you need.

Step 3: Calculate the Solar Array Size

Sizing Formula

  • 📊 Formula: Daily kWh usage ÷ Peak sun hours = Required kW of solar panels
  • 📝 Example: 30 kWh/day ÷ 5 peak sun hours = 6 kW system
  • ➕ Add 20–25% to account for system inefficiencies and losses

Step 4: Determine Battery Storage

If you're going off-grid or want backup power, you'll need battery storage. Decide how many days of autonomy you need (typically 1–3 days).

Battery Sizing Formula

  • 🔋 Formula: Daily kWh usage × Days of autonomy ÷ Battery depth of discharge
  • 📝 Example: 30 kWh × 2 days ÷ 0.8 DoD = 75 kWh of battery storage

Step 5: Size the Inverter

The inverter should match or slightly exceed your solar array output in kW. For battery systems, the inverter must also handle your peak load demand. Consider a hybrid inverter for grid-tied systems with battery backup.

Common Sizing Mistakes to Avoid

Watch Out For

  • ⚠️ Underestimating future energy needs (EV charging, HVAC upgrades)
  • 📈 Using only average usage without accounting for peak demand
  • 🌳 Ignoring shading from trees or roofline obstructions
  • 🌡️ Forgetting to factor in temperature derating for panels in hot climates
