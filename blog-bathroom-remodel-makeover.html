Five bathroom transformations, one video — this showcase of before and after renovations is packed with design ideas for every style and budget. Whether you want a simple refresh or a full gut remodel, these real-world examples will help you visualize the possibilities.

The 5 Renovation Designs

1. Small Bathroom Transformation

  • 🚿 Replaced cramped tub with a sleek walk-in shower
  • 🧱 Light-colored large-format tile to make the space feel bigger
  • 🪞 Floating vanity with under-cabinet lighting

2. Master Bathroom Luxury Upgrade

  • 🛁 Freestanding soaking tub as the centerpiece
  • 🪟 Custom double vanity with quartz countertops
  • 🏛️ Floor-to-ceiling marble-look porcelain tile

3. Budget-Friendly Guest Bathroom Refresh

  • 💡 New vanity, mirror, and light fixture — no plumbing moved
  • 🧱 Peel-and-stick floor tile for an affordable floor update
  • 🎨 Fresh paint and new accessories for a complete new look

4. Vintage Bathroom Modernization

  • 🚫 Removed pink tile and dated fixtures
  • 🧱 Installed subway tile with dark grout for a timeless look
  • 🖤 Matte black fixtures throughout

5. Accessible Bathroom Remodel

  • 🚿 Walk-in shower with zero-threshold entry
  • 🦺 Grab bars integrated into the tile design
  • 🧱 Non-slip floor tile and comfort-height toilet

Key Design Tips from All 5 Renovations

Expert Takeaways

  • 🎨 Tile selection is the most impactful design decision in any bathroom remodel
  • 💡 Lighting layers (overhead, vanity, accent) dramatically elevate the feel
  • 🔧 Consistent finishes across fixtures and hardware create a cohesive look
  • 📦 Storage solutions should be planned from the start, not added as an afterthought
