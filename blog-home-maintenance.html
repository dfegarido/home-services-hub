Choosing a Ring Doorbell in 2026 | The Ultimate Guide to Ring Video Doorbells

Watch on YouTube

Choosing the right video doorbell can significantly enhance your home security. With a variety of products to consider, it's essential to understand their features and differences. In this comprehensive guide, we'll explore the complete range of Ring video doorbells, including battery-powered, wired options, and specialty models for apartments.

Why Choose Ring Video Doorbells?

Ring has established itself as a leader in the smart home security market, offering reliable video doorbells that provide peace of mind and convenience. Whether you're protecting packages, monitoring visitors, or enhancing your home's security, Ring offers solutions for every need and budget.

Battery-Powered Ring Doorbells

Ring's battery doorbells offer a convenient installation option with their easy-to-change battery system. These are perfect for homes without existing doorbell wiring or renters who need a non-permanent solution.

1. Ring Battery Doorbell Plus

Key Features

  • 📐 1x1 Aspect Ratio: Unique square format for better coverage
  • 📹 1536p Resolution: Crystal-clear video quality
  • 📦 Full-Body Visibility: See visitors head-to-toe and packages on the ground
  • 🔋 Removable Battery: Easy charging without removing the entire unit
  • 💰 Price: ~$180

2. Ring Battery Doorbell (2024 Version)

As an economical option, the 2024 version features a 1440p resolution with a compact design, although the battery isn't removable.

Key Features

  • 📹 1440p Resolution: High-definition video
  • 📱 Compact Design: Sleek and modern appearance
  • 🔋 Built-in Battery: Charges via micro-USB (not removable)
  • 💰 Price: ~$100 (Most affordable option)
  • ✅ Best For: Budget-conscious homeowners

3. Ring Battery Doorbell Pro

This is the premium battery-powered option with advanced features like 3D motion detection and bird's eye view, though it's at a higher price point.

Key Features

  • 🎯 3D Motion Detection: Pinpoint exactly where motion occurs
  • 🦅 Bird's Eye View: Aerial map showing visitor paths
  • 📹 1536p HD+ Resolution: Premium video quality
  • 🔋 Dual Battery Support: Swap batteries for continuous operation
  • 💰 Price: ~$230
  • ⭐ Best For: Tech enthusiasts wanting premium features
The price points range from $100 to $230, which vary based on features and desired video quality. Experts suggest considering which features are truly necessary for your needs to avoid unnecessary spending.

Wired Ring Doorbells

For homes with existing doorbell wiring, wired options provide constant power without the need for battery changes. These are ideal for homeowners who want a set-it-and-forget-it solution.

1. Ring Video Doorbell Wired

Key Features

  • 💵 Ultra-Affordable: Only $50
  • 🔌 Hardwired Power: Never worry about charging
  • 📹 1080p Video: Clear HD resolution
  • 📏 16:9 Aspect Ratio: Traditional wide format
  • ⚠️ Limitation: Limited vertical field of view for packages
  • ✅ Best For: Extreme budget constraints with existing wiring

2. Ring Video Doorbell Pro

Retailing at $230, it offers premium features and a compact design, making it suitable for those seeking high performance in a wired setup.

Key Features

  • 🎨 Sleek Design: Slim, modern aesthetic
  • 📹 1536p HD Resolution: Premium video quality
  • 🎯 Advanced Motion Detection: Customizable zones
  • 🔌 Hardwired: Constant power
  • 💰 Price: ~$230
  • ⭐ Best For: Homeowners wanting premium wired performance

Installation Note: Keep in mind, professional installation may be advisable for complex electrical setups to ensure code compliance and safety. Most installations can be DIY-friendly with basic electrical knowledge.

Ring Peephole Cam (For Apartments)

Specifically designed for apartment use, the Ring Peephole Cam replaces your existing peephole. With useful features like knock detection, it provides practical security solutions where traditional doorbells can't be installed.

Key Features

  • 🚪 Peephole Replacement: Fits standard door peepholes
  • 👊 Knock Detection: Alerts when someone knocks
  • 🔋 Battery Powered: No wiring required
  • 📹 1080p Video: HD quality
  • 📏 16:9 Aspect Ratio: May limit package viewing
  • 🏢 Best For: Apartment dwellers and renters
  • 💰 Price: ~$130

  • Best Features: Ring Battery Doorbell Pro ($230)

Ring Subscription Service

Ring's subscription service, priced between $5-$20 per month, amplifies the functionality of all Ring devices, providing extended video history and additional security features.

Ring Protect Plans

  • 💲 Basic Plan ($5/month): 180-day video history for one device
  • 💲 Plus Plan ($10/month): All devices at one location
  • 💲 Pro Plan ($20/month): Extended warranty + 24/7 professional monitoring
  • 📹 Includes: Video recording, sharing, and downloading
  • 🔔 Bonus Features: Rich notifications, person alerts, extended warranty

Evaluating the value of subscription services is crucial based on the scope of intended surveillance and budget. Without a subscription, you'll still get live view and motion alerts, but won't be able to review recorded footage.

"For comprehensive home security, pairing Ring devices with a subscription service might offer the most practical solution," experts recommend.

Installation Tips

Best Practices

  • 📏 Optimal Height: Install 48 inches from the ground
  • 📶 Check Wi-Fi: Ensure strong signal at installation location
  • 🔧 Use Level: Ensure doorbell is straight for proper viewing angle
  • ⚡ Test Wiring: For wired models, verify 16-24V AC transformer
  • 🌦️ Weather Protection: Consider corner kit for rain protection
  • 🔔 Chime Compatibility: Check if existing chime needs a Ring Chime Pro

Additional Considerations

Wi-Fi Requirements

  • Minimum 2 Mbps upload speed (per Ring device)
  • 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz network (some models 2.4 GHz only)
  • Consider Ring Chime Pro for Wi-Fi extending

Privacy and Security

  • Enable two-factor authentication
  • Adjust motion zones to respect neighbor privacy
  • Use privacy zones to block sensitive areas
  • Regularly update app and firmware

Integration with Smart Home

  • Works with Alexa for voice control and announcements
  • Compatible with Ring security systems
  • Integrates with Ring Chime and Chime Pro
  • Can trigger smart lights and other Ring devices

Warranty and Support

All Ring doorbells come with:

  • 1-year limited warranty (standard)
  • Extended warranty with Ring Protect Plus/Pro
  • 24/7 customer support
  • Theft protection (free replacement if stolen)

Ready to Enhance Your Home Security?

Choose the Ring video doorbell that best fits your needs, budget, and home setup. Whether you opt for the budget-friendly wired option at $50 or the feature-packed Battery Doorbell Pro at $230, you'll be taking an important step toward protecting your home and loved ones. Don't forget to consider a Ring Protect subscription to maximize your device's capabilities!

Final Verdict

Ring offers an excellent range of video doorbells suitable for various needs:

  • For Budget Buyers: Ring Video Doorbell Wired ($50) or Ring Battery Doorbell ($100)
  • For Best Features: Ring Battery Doorbell Pro ($230)
  • For Package Monitoring: Ring Battery Doorbell Plus ($180) with 1:1 aspect ratio
  • For Apartments: Ring Peephole Cam ($130)
  • For Wired Homes: Ring Video Doorbell Pro ($230)

The key is matching the features to your specific needs. Not everyone needs 3D motion detection or bird's eye view, so carefully consider which features matter most to you. Combined with a Ring Protect subscription, any of these doorbells can significantly enhance your home's security and convenience.
