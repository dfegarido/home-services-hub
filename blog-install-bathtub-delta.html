What You Will Need

Tools & Materials

  • 🧤 Work gloves and safety glasses

Step-by-Step Installation

Step 1: Prepare the Alcove

Before the tub goes in, confirm that the three walls of the alcove are plumb and the floor is level. Check that the rough drain in the floor aligns with the bathtub's drain location. Install blocking between studs at the tub flange height — this gives you a solid nailing surface to secure the tub.

Step 2: Dry-Fit the Tub

Slide the tub into the alcove without adhesive or mortar to confirm it fits correctly. Check that the drain lines up with the floor drain rough-in and that the tub is level side-to-side and end-to-end. Mark the tub flange position on the studs with a pencil.

Step 3: Set the Mortar Bed

Mix a dry-pack mortar and spread it evenly across the floor of the alcove where the tub will sit. This mortar bed supports the bottom of the tub and prevents flexing and cracking over time. Some installers use adjustable support legs instead — follow the tub manufacturer's recommendation.

Step 4: Set the Tub and Check Level

Carefully lower the tub into the alcove onto the mortar bed. Press it down evenly and immediately check level in all directions. Make any adjustments before the mortar begins to set. Do not stand in the tub during this step.

Step 5: Connect the Drain Assembly

Install the drain body through the tub floor, connecting it to the floor drain rough-in with PVC fittings, cement, and primer. Install the overflow plate and linkage on the end wall of the tub. Test all connections for leaks before closing up the walls.

Step 6: Secure the Tub Flange

Once the mortar has cured and the tub is confirmed level, nail or screw the tub flange to the wall studs through the pre-drilled holes. Do not overtighten — snug is sufficient. The flange holds the top edge of the tub firmly against the wall framing.

Step 7: Install Backer Board and Surround

Install cement backer board or the Delta Classic 400 surround panels over the tub flange and up the walls. Follow the surround installation guide for adhesive application and panel alignment. Keep a 1/8" gap between the bottom of the panels and the tub rim for the caulk joint.

Step 8: Caulk All Seams

Apply a continuous bead of 100% silicone caulk along the joint where the tub meets the walls, and at all panel seams and corners. Smooth the caulk with a wet finger or caulk tool. Allow to cure fully (24–48 hours) before exposing to water.

Pro Installation Tips

Get the Best Results

  • 🧹 Clean the alcove floor thoroughly before spreading mortar
  • 📐 Double-check level at every step — a tub that isn't level will drain poorly and look off
  • 💧 Always pressure-test drain connections before closing up walls
  • 🔒 Use 100% silicone caulk (not latex) for the tub-to-wall joint — it's fully waterproof and flexible
  • 🏊 Fill the tub with water before caulking — this compresses the tub at its maximum weight, preventing the caulk from pulling away later
  • 📅 Wait the full mortar and caulk cure time before first use
